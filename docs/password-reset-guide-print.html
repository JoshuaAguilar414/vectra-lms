How to get a new password

A very simple guide. Read it slowly. Do one step at a time.

Stop. Read this first.

Have you signed in on this website before?

  • Yes — good. Follow the steps below.
  • No — stop. Do not click Forgot password. Go to the sign-in page and click click here to make an account first.

What you need

  • Your work email (the same one you used before)
  • You can open that email
  • About 5 minutes

Do these 7 steps

1

Open the website

Go to https://lms-otto-group.onrender.com/login

You should see the words Welcome back.

2

Click Forgot password?

Look under the big red Sign in button. Click the words Forgot password?

3

Type your email

Type your work email. Click the red button Send reset link.

A message will appear. That is OK. Keep going.

4

Open your email

Look for a message called Reset your Otto Group Academy password.

Cannot see it? Wait 2 minutes. Then look in Spam, Junk, or Quarantine.

This email is only good for 2 hours. If you wait too long, go back to Step 1.

5

Click the link

In the email, click Reset your password. A new page will open. That is the right page.

6

Make a new secret password

Type a new password in the first box. Type the same password in the second box. Click Reset password.

Make your password like this:

at least 10 letters and numbers long  ·  one BIG letter like A  ·  one small letter like a  ·  one number like 1

Good: Sunshine2026    Not good: hello

7

Sign in

Click Go to sign in. Type your work email and your new password. Click Sign in.

You are done. Well done.

If you get stuck

I cannot find the email. Look in Spam, Junk, or Quarantine. Check you typed the right work email.

The link does not work. It is too old. The link only lasts 2 hours. Start again from Step 1.

It says the passwords do not match. Type the same new password in both boxes. Type it slowly.

It will not take my password. Make it longer. Add a BIG letter, a small letter, and a number. Try Sunshine2026.

I have never signed in before. Do not use Forgot password. Register first.
